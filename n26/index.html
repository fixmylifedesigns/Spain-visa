Banking · N26

Our Spanish bank: N26

Irving's N26 account is already open with our NIE, so we arrive with a working Spanish account. Here's what it does for us and what to update after the move.

What we get

  • A Spanish IBAN starting with ES. N26 has given Spanish IBANs to accounts opened in Spain since April 2019, so landlords, utilities and employers treat it as a local account.
  • Direct debits for rent, phone, electricity and insurance.
  • Bizum for instant payments to people by phone number, which is how many landlords and friends in Spain get paid.
  • Deposits protected up to €100,000.
  • Once we're Spanish residents with a local tax ID, the Instant Savings space becomes available.
N26 Spain

Taxes

N26 says an account with a Spanish ES IBAN counts as a Spanish account, so it doesn't need to be declared as a foreign account. A German DE IBAN would. Check that our IBAN starts with ES.

Our U.S. accounts are a separate question for the tax adviser once we become Spanish tax residents.

N26 support: declaring the account

Visa bank statements: the DNV needs three months of statements that show salary deposits matching the pay stubs. Keep salary landing in the same account through filing, and don't move payroll to N26 before then without asking our lawyer.

After the move

  • Update our address to the Valencia flat once we're on the padrón.
  • Add the TIE number and update tax residency in the app when we become Spanish tax residents.
  • Moeno can open her own N26 once she has her NIE or TIE.
  • The TIE fee (Modelo 790, código 012) is usually paid at a bank. Check whether N26 can pay it; if not, pay at a collaborating bank.

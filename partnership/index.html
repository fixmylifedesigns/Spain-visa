California registered domestic partnership

Our relationship document for Spain

Moeno applies as Irving's family member, so we need to prove our relationship. Our plan is to use a California registered domestic partnership instead of registering a Spanish pareja de hecho.

Why California instead of pareja de hecho

  • Spain accepts a registered union. The UGE guidance for family members lists a certificate of registration of the partnership in a public registry. California's Domestic Partners Registry is a public state registry.
  • A Spanish pareja de hecho needs us to live there first. Regional registries generally require padrón in that region, so we couldn't register before filing in Málaga, and it would tie us to one city.
  • Without any registration, we'd have to prove at least one continuous year of living together right before applying, with several kinds of evidence like joint accounts or a shared lease.
  • Lawyers note that a registered partner can be covered as a beneficiary, while an unregistered partner must bring separate health insurance.
  • We can do the whole thing from Japan: sign, notarize and file by mail.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Fill in form DP-1

    The California Declaration of Domestic Partnership. We both sign the same original.

  2. 2

    Notarize both signatures

    Signatures can be notarized separately, including abroad through an accepted notary route.

  3. 3

    File with the Secretary of State

    Mail or courier the original with the current state fee.

  4. 4

    Order a certified copy

    The file-stamped certified declaration is what Spain needs, not a decorative certificate.

  5. 5

    California apostille

    The Secretary of State apostilles the certified copy.

  6. 6

    Sworn Spanish translation

    A traductor jurado translates the whole apostilled packet, after the apostille, never before.

  7. 7

    File with Moeno's application

    It becomes the proof of our relationship in her family-member application.
